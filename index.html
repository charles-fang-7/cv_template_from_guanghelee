I am especially interested in battery materials, chemical-looping and thermochemical materials, and autonomous materials experimentation. Beyond the lab, I enjoy outdoor training, hiking, Chinese traditional culture, and Wushu.

03 / Selected projects

Two early projects at the boundary of materials, devices, and modelling.

01

National Key R&D Program · Sep 2020 — Jun 2021

High-conductivity metal-matrix composites ↗

Developed molybdenum–copper composites and an ultrathin (<1 mm) high-performance heat-pipe platform. The work raised thermal conductivity toward 10,000 W/(m·K), supporting thermal management for high-power-density multi-finger-gate GaN transistors.

  • MATLAB
  • COMSOL
  • Infrared thermography
  • High-speed imaging
02
Al

Multiphysics simulation · Mar 2021 — Sep 2021

Aluminum-alloy additive manufacturing ↗

Combined temperature-data collection with coupled heat-transfer and fluid-flow simulations to study grain formation, melt-pool impact, and solidification. The resulting 2D and 3D models informed printing-rate, cooling, and tool-path optimization.

  • COMSOL
  • Heat transfer
  • Fluid dynamics
  • Additive manufacturing
